About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at IIIT Delhi, where I work on designing algorithms that make smart decisions under uncertainty. My research focuses on online algorithms and approximation algorithms—essentially, how to make good choices when you don't have all the information upfront and can't afford to wait for perfect solutions.

My journey into algorithms started during my time at ISI Kolkata, where I worked on geometric problems like finding optimal guard positions on terrains and computing shortest paths while avoiding obstacles. There's something deeply satisfying about turning geometric intuition into rigorous algorithms. These days, I'm drawn to matching and transportation problems, questions like how to efficiently pair ride-sharing requests with drivers or match supplies with demands when requests arrive in real-time.

I've been lucky to learn from and collaborate with wonderful mentors: Prof. Syamantak Das at IIIT Delhi, Prof. Subhas C. Nandy at ISI Kolkata, Prof. Anil Maheshwari at Carleton University, and Prof. Sagnik Sen at IIT Dharwad. Our work has appeared at venues like ICLR, Computational Geometry: Theory and Applications, and CCCG.
